DouScan 鸿蒙版隐私政策

更新日期：2026-10-08 · 开发者：王晋（wjkkal）

简体中文 · English

本政策适用于“二维码与条码扫描器 - DouScan”鸿蒙版（包名 vip.wangdoudou.qr）。隐私联系邮箱：wangjin8909@163.com。

1. 扫码、生成与历史记录

应用在你的设备上处理相机画面、主动选择的图片、扫码结果和二维码生成内容。扫码内容、解析结果、生成内容和历史记录不上传至我们的服务器。历史、收藏和扫描批次保存在本机；核心扫码与生成功能无需登录，可以离线使用。

你主动导出 CSV、分享二维码或打开系统联系人、日历编辑页面时，所选目标应用或系统功能会接收相应内容。是否保存或分享由你确认，接收方按其自身隐私政策处理数据。

2. 网络访问与可选反馈

应用不会自动访问扫码得到的网址。你确认打开网址后，浏览器会连接相应网站，网站可能接收网络请求信息，并按其自身隐私政策处理数据。

只有你主动提交意见反馈时，我们的反馈服务才接收你填写的文字、反馈类别、应用在本机生成的标识，以及你自愿填写的邮箱或电话号码。这些信息用于处理问题及回复，不自动附带扫码内容。请勿在反馈中填写不必要的敏感信息。

当前鸿蒙发布版本不在启动时自动上传设备注册或使用日志，不展示广告，不启用广告追踪。反馈使用 HTTPS 传输；我们不出售你的扫码内容或反馈信息。

3. 权限与系统功能

相机用于实时扫码。图片选择用于图片扫码或添加 Logo，仅处理你主动选择的图片。网络用于主动打开网址及提交反馈。振动用于扫码提示。联系人和日历内容通过你主动打开的系统编辑页面确认保存；应用不读取你的通讯录或日历列表，不使用定位权限。

4. 保存、删除与隐私权利

本地历史可在应用内删除；卸载应用会移除其本地应用数据。你已导出或分享到其它位置的文件需要自行删除。反馈信息仅用于反馈处理及必要的后续沟通；你可通过上述邮箱请求查询、更正或删除与应用标识或联系方式关联的反馈，也可停止使用可选反馈功能。

5. 未成年人、政策变更与联系

应用不专门面向儿童，也不要求提供年龄、姓名等信息。未成年人使用网络及反馈功能时应由监护人指导。若应用功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策及更新日期。如有隐私问题，请联系上述邮箱。

DouScan for HarmonyOS Privacy Policy

Updated: October 8, 2026 · Developer: Wang Jin (wjkkal)

This policy applies to DouScan for HarmonyOS (bundle vip.wangdoudou.qr). Privacy contact: wangjin8909@163.com.

1. Scanning, generation and history

Camera frames, selected images, scan results and generated codes are processed on your device. We do not upload scanned content, parsed results, generated content or history to our servers. History, favorites and scan batches are stored locally. Core scanning and generation work offline without an account.

When you choose to export CSV, share a code, or open a system contact or calendar editor, the selected app or system function receives the corresponding content. You decide whether to save or share it. Recipients process data under their own privacy policies.

2. Network access and optional feedback

The app does not automatically visit scanned URLs. After you confirm opening a URL, the browser connects to that website. The website may receive network request information and processes it under its own privacy policy.

Only when you submit feedback does our feedback service receive the text you enter, feedback category, an identifier generated locally by the app, and an email address or phone number you optionally provide. We use this information to investigate and respond. Scanned content is not automatically attached. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in feedback.

The current HarmonyOS release does not automatically upload device registration or usage logs at startup, display ads, or enable advertising tracking. Feedback is transmitted over HTTPS. We do not sell your scanned content or feedback information.

3. Permissions and system functions

The camera is used for live scanning. Image selection is used for scanning an image or adding a logo; the app only processes images you select. Network access is used for websites you open and feedback you submit. Vibration provides scan feedback. Contacts and calendar events are saved through system editors that you choose to open and confirm. The app does not read your contact or calendar lists or use location permission.

4. Storage, deletion and privacy requests

You can delete local history in the app. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. Files you have exported or shared elsewhere must be deleted separately. Feedback is used only to handle your request and necessary follow-up communication. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of feedback linked to your app identifier or contact details. Feedback is optional.

5. Children, changes and contact

The app is not specifically directed at children and does not require age or name information. Minors should use network and feedback features with guidance from a guardian. If functionality or data handling changes, we will update this policy and its date. Contact the email above with privacy questions.
